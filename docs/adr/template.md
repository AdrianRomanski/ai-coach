# ADR-NNNN: Short imperative statement of the decision

## Context

What forces the decision: the problem, the constraints, and what is true today.

## Decision

What we do, stated as rules a reader can check against the code.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what is now required.
